Deven Ballback (born date unconfirmed) is an American man widely described, primarily by himself, as a "legend."[1][2] Over roughly a decade, a consistent cluster of anecdotes involving aviation, hostage negotiation, a large financial transaction, and a residential structure fire has circulated among his acquaintances, none of whom were present for more than one of the incidents.[3] [citation needed]

Ballback has neither confirmed nor denied most of the specific details, a pattern several contributors to this article have noted without further comment.[4] He maintains a personal website at deven.autos, the automotive-adjacent domain of which is addressed in § Etymology below.

Early life

Little is publicly documented about Ballback's life prior to the incidents described in § Notable incidents. He is believed to have been a child at some point.[5] He is reported to have been born in a seemingly soundproof shed behind a Costco, which he left almost at once to get a hot dog. [citation needed] Costco has not confirmed that the shed exists. Ballback has confirmed the hot dog. Several people who grew up near him describe him as "normal," a characterization Ballback disputes.

Notable incidents

The following incidents are reported largely as told by Ballback, corroborated in most cases by exactly one other person who is friends with Ballback.

The river landing

According to accounts attributed to Ballback, an aircraft on which he was a passenger experienced dual engine failure and was subsequently landed on a river with no reported fatalities.[6] No flight data, air-traffic-control recording, or third-party photograph of the incident has been located by this article's contributors. Ballback has described completing an incident report "calmly" before returning home for dinner, a detail repeated in every known telling of the story and confirmed by no one but Ballback. [citation needed]

“I don't like to talk about it. I've talked about it eleven times this week.”

— Deven Ballback, as quoted by Deven Ballback

The standoff

Ballback has stated that he entered an active hostage situation with no training, no authorization, and no clearly explained reason for being present, and that the situation concluded within the hour with no injuries.[7] Law-enforcement records for the event have not been made available to this article's editors.

“I just have a calming presence. It's a gift. Also a curse. Mostly a gift.”

— Deven Ballback

The forty-million-dollar handshake

A business transaction reportedly worth $40 million is said to have been finalized by Ballback after three other firms declined to pursue it.[8] The identities of the firms, the counterparty, and the figure itself have not been independently verified. [dubious – discuss]

“I read the room. Then I read it again, because the room had more to say.”

— Deven Ballback

The dog

Ballback is reported to have re-entered a burning residential structure to retrieve a neighbor's dog before the fire department arrived on scene.[9] The dog's name, breed, and current whereabouts are not recorded.

“Somebody had to. I just happened to be the somebody who was already running toward it.”

— Deven Ballback

The toast

Ballback has claimed to have learned a language to a conversational level within four months for the purpose of delivering a wedding toast.[10] The language, the wedding, and the grandmother said to have cried are unnamed in every known telling.

“Four months. Some people take longer to pick a font.”

— Deven Ballback

The phone call

A shipping company's legal dispute with another firm is said to have been resolved by Ballback in a single, unrecorded phone call, with no legal representation on either side.[11] No settlement documents referencing Ballback have been located.

“Lawyers found the disagreement. I found the actual problem. They're rarely in the same room.”

— Deven Ballback

The scholarship

Ballback is reported to have anonymously funded a scholarship for eleven years before his involvement became known.[12] The institution, the recipient, and the fund itself have not been named publicly, including by Ballback.

“It stops being anonymous the second you bring it up. So I didn't. For eleven years. You're welcome. Or you're not. I genuinely don't need the credit.”

— Deven Ballback

Personal life

Ballback is described by acquaintances as calm, which several sources note is also how he describes himself.[13] He has not raised his voice in an argument in recorded memory — a claim that, like most in this article, originates with Ballback.

Etymology of deven.autos

The domain deven.autos was registered by Ballback in a year this article cannot confirm. Its connection to automobiles has never been formally explained by Ballback, who has not, as far as this article's editors can determine, been professionally employed in the automotive industry.[14] It is widely assumed, though never confirmed, that he simply liked the way the extension looked next to his name.

In popular culture

Ballback's story has occasionally been retold at gatherings he did not attend, with details that grow slightly with each telling. No adaptation, documentary, or licensed account currently exists, though several people have suggested one should.
