The Legend, chronologically

A partial, heavily-corroborated timeline.

Chapter I

Landed the plane after both engines failed, on a river, with no warning

Ninety seconds from windshield full of birds to a water landing every passenger walked away from. He filled out the incident report calmly and went home for dinner.

Chapter II

Walked into a hostage situation and walked everyone out an hour later

No training, no authority to be there. Just stayed calm, kept talking, and somehow became the person both sides trusted.

Chapter III

Closed a forty-million-dollar deal that three other firms had already walked away from

Read the room, found the actual sticking point nobody else had bothered to ask about, and had it signed inside a week.

Chapter IV

Ran back into a burning building for a neighbor's dog while the fire department was still pulling up

Came out coughing, dog fine, refused to be called anything more than "lucky" about it.

Chapter V

Learned a language well enough to give a wedding toast in it, in under four months

The groom's grandmother cried. She doesn't speak a word of English. Neither, technically, did Deven four months earlier.

Chapter VI

Is, to this day, still the person everyone calls first with good news

Not because he's the closest. Because he's the one who'll actually sound excited — right after whatever he's in the middle of fixing.

Recognized talents

Skills acquired through sheer force of personality.

Talent one

Staying Calm Under Real Pressure

The engines-out, hostage-situation, room-going-sideways kind of pressure. Somehow gets quieter and more decisive the worse things get.

Talent two

Reading the Actual Problem

Finds the real sticking point in a negotiation, a fight, or a deal everyone else already gave up on — usually in about ten minutes.

Talent three

Picking Things Up Fast

Languages, instruments, whatever the moment calls for. Not a prodigy — just puts in absurd hours and never mentions it.
