Mode: Persuade (shareable public gag page; the "conversion" is landing the joke and getting a guestbook signature/share).

Audience: strangers with zero prior context, receiving the link cold.
Job: read the bit, get it within seconds, optionally sign the guestbook.
Constraints: giscus guestbook mechanism must keep working (same repo mapping); CNAME preserved; fish.html untouched; copy must be rewritten to be less "cringe" — funnier through understatement/dry skepticism, not sincere gushing.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site refuses the "prestige landing page" arrangement (hero + CTA + stat bar + testimonials + awards grid) the old version already used, and instead commits fully to a faithful Wikipedia "notable person" article parody — real encyclopedia chrome, not a stylized homage. The joke is structural: an encyclopedia's dry, skeptical, citation-hungry voice reporting a private citizen's self-mythologized legend as contested fact. Copy voice channels Barney-Stinson-style grandiose self-mythologizing (told in his own grandiose register within quoted claims) undercut by the encyclopedia's third-person skepticism and "[citation needed]" tags — that mismatch replaces the old version's sincere-prestige cringe.

OWN-WORLD: Real Wikipedia visual grammar, committed to its native interface language rather than a loose homage — white article ground, black serif body text (Georgia/Linux-Libertine-style for the page title, Helvetica-Neue/Arial-style sans for body and UI chrome, matching real Wikipedia), Wikipedia blue links (#3366cc/#0645ad), thin #a2a9b1 hairline rules, light gray #eaecf0 infobox/table headers, superscript bracketed citations [1][2][3], a maintenance "multiple issues" banner box, an auto-generated-look Table of Contents box, a right-floated infobox card (photo, Born, Known for, Occupation, Website), a References list, and a Categories footer strip. Decorative (non-functional) top utility bar and Article/Talk/Read/Edit/View-history tabs for authenticity.

STORY: Visitor lands on what looks exactly like a real Wikipedia article mid-edit-war (a maintenance banner flags it as reading "like it was written by the subject's biggest fan"). The lead paragraph and infobox play it straight. Section by section ("Early life", "Notable incidents", "Personal life", "Awards and honors", "In popular culture", "Talk page") the legend beats from the old copy are rewritten in encyclopedic voice with skeptical citations, "[dubious – discuss]" tags, and one self-authored quote per section in Deven's own grandiose first-person voice (block-quoted, attributed "— Deven Ballback, in a 2019 email to himself"). The References list is itself a punchline (sources like "Personal recollection, unverified", "Ballback, D. (self-published)"). The guestbook becomes the article's == Talk page == section, giscus embedded under real Talk-page styling with a mock "(UTC)" signature joke.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full Wikipedia article chrome from the top — thin gray utility bar, logo-less wordmark area, search box replica, left sidebar nav (reflavored: "Main page", "Legendary feats", "Talk", "Random legend"), Article/Talk/Read/Edit/View-history tab row, the maintenance banner box immediately below, then the page title "Deven Ballback" in the serif display face with an italic one-line description under it ("American man, subject of ongoing dispute regarding the veracity of his own legend"), lead paragraph with inline citations wrapping around the right-floated infobox (photo placeholder, Born/Known for/Occupation/Website fields), auto-TOC box below the lead.

FORM: Wikipedia "notable person" article parody — locked by direct user choice (Impeccable's Pick card from the direction roll, seed key 0da3542a), overriding the rolled assignment (mourning-card/memorial-program direction) per "a user-pinned direction beats the roll, always."

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
